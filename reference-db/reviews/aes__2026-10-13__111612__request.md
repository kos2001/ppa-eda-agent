### fanout_loads.py  (matched signoff:antenna, signoff:max-fanout)

- **answers**: What the loads on each over-fanout net are, and what the fanout would be without antenna diodes.
- **run**: `python3 pipeline/fanout_loads.py --run-dir <run>`
- **trap**: Turning the fanout limit up makes the number go away by editing the test. Look at the loads first: diode insertion (RUN_HEURISTIC_DIODE_INSERTION, GRT antenna repair) runs AFTER the resizer's last fanout repair, so every diode it adds is a load nothing then fixes. The clock tree is affected too: diodes land on clock nets.
- **on the record**: sram_wrapper: 486 diodes, 10 of 15 listed violators were within the limit without them (e.g. 17 loads, 9 of them diodes). Turning heuristic insertion off took max-fanout 18 -> 5 with antenna violations still 0-1. aes shows the same coupling: antenna 15 -> 8 as fanout went 4 -> 16.

### lib_slew_floor.py, then lib_chain.py  (matched signoff:max-slew)

- **answers**: Whether any standard-cell driver can meet a slew limit at all at a given corner, and which taper gets closest - from the liberty's own tables, before a 4-minute run.
- **run**: `python3 pipeline/lib_slew_floor.py --lib <corner>.lib --cells buf_16 clkinv_16 --load 0.009 && python3 pipeline/lib_chain.py --lib <corner>.lib --chain clkinv_2 clkinv_4 clkinv_8 clkinv_16 --load 0.009`
- **trap**: Three. A buffer is two stages and its output edge is slow: at ss_100C into a macro pin (9 fF) buf_16 gives 0.048 ns and clkinv_16 gives 0.026, so the last stage that can meet a tight limit is an inverter. The macro's liberty measures slew 10-90% where the standard cells measure 20-80%, so STA reports the same edge 1.333x slower at the macro pin than at the driver (0.0588 ns out, 0.0785 ns in, on 28 ohm of wire). And a strong last stage still gives a slow edge when what feeds it is slow: clkinv_16 is 0.032 ns on a sharp input and 0.059 on a 0.24 ns one.
- **on the record**: sram_wrapper: RSZ-0090's 'best achievable 0.043 ns' is the best BUFFER. With per-port counters and a four-stage inverter taper the address pins came to 0.039-0.050 ns at ss against 0.07-0.12 before, which is the edge of what this library can do (experiments/README.md).

### macro_pins.py, then io_driver_report.py  (matched signoff:max-slew)

- **answers**: Which side of the block every pin is on, where it is in the die, and how far the cell that drives it ended up.
- **run**: `python3 pipeline/macro_pins.py --lef <macro>.lef --origin X Y --prefix addr0 addr1`
- **trap**: Pin NAMES say nothing about sides. The SRAM's addr0[0] is on its bottom edge and addr0[1..7] on its left; addr1 is spread over the top, right and bottom. An earlier note in this repo grouped pins by prefix ('addr0 left, addr1 right') and an orientation grid was reasoned from it.
- **on the record**: sram_wrapper: the finished run's address drivers sat 60-157 um from their pins once the resizer had shrunk them to inv_4; io_driver_report.py prints that distance per pin.
