# Reading Synopsys Design Compiler `report_area`

## Where it comes from

Generated by Design Compiler (DC) via the `report_area` TCL command, usually
run after `compile_ultra` (or `compile`/`link`) on a synthesized netlist. It
reflects the *synthesis-stage* estimate of cell area, before place & route —
real post-layout area (from Innovus/ICC2) can differ due to routing
congestion fixes, buffer insertion during CTS, and filler/decap cells.

## Typical structure

```
Report : area
Design : top_module
Version: T-2022.03-SP5
Date   : ...

Library(s) Used:
    saed32rvt_ss1p05v0c (File: ...)

Number of ports:                          312
Number of nets:                          8842
Number of cells:                         7615
Number of combinational cells:           5203
Number of sequential cells:              2107
Number of macros/black boxes:               2
Number of buf/inv:                       1108

Total combinational area:      12045.238400
Total noncombinational area:   18932.556800
Total buf/inv area:             2210.995200 (included above)
Total macro/black box area:    45210.880000
Net Interconnect area:          undefined (Wire load model not compiled)

Total cell area:               76188.675200
Total area:                    undefined
```

## Key metrics and where they live

- **Total cell area** = combinational + noncombinational + macro/black box
  area. This is the number to track for area budget compliance.
- **Total noncombinational area** — flip-flop/latch area. A high ratio of
  noncombinational-to-combinational area often means a register-heavy
  microarchitecture (deep pipelining, wide state) rather than a synthesis
  problem — check against the RTL intent before "optimizing" it away.
- **Total buf/inv area** (subset of combinational) — buffers and inverters
  inserted by DC for timing/drive-strength reasons. A large fraction here
  relative to total combinational area often indicates DC is fighting long
  nets or high fanout; this is a signal to check `report_timing` for the
  paths driving that buffering, not just an area problem in isolation.
- **Number of macros/black boxes** and their area — SRAMs, hard IP, or
  unresolved black boxes. If this dominates total area (as in the example
  above), synthesis-driven area reduction on standard cells will barely
  move total area — the real lever is macro selection/floorplanning, out of
  DC's scope.
- **Net Interconnect area** — only populated when a wire-load model is
  compiled in; frequently `undefined` in modern flows since P&R tools
  compute real wire area instead. Don't treat "undefined" as an error.

## Common patterns and what they mean

- **Area grew after a timing fix**: expected if the fix was upsizing cells
  or adding buffers/pipeline stages — check `report_timing` on the same
  paths to confirm the area increase bought back slack, and treat it as a
  deliberate PPA trade rather than a regression.
- **buf/inv area is a large fraction of combinational area**: look for high-
  fanout nets or long combinational paths in `report_timing`; consider
  restructuring logic (e.g. balancing a wide mux tree) instead of relying on
  DC to keep buffering the same structure every run.
- **Sequential area much larger than expected for the RTL**: check for
  accidental latch inference (should be flip-flops) or redundant
  reset/clock-domain-crossing synchronizer chains — both inflate
  noncombinational area without design intent.
- **Total area is `undefined`**: normal when interconnect area isn't
  computed; use "Total cell area" as the tracked metric instead.

## Relationship to the other PPA legs

- **Area ↔ Timing**: upsizing cells or adding buffer/pipeline stages to fix
  timing (see `report-timing.md`) almost always costs area. Multi-Vt
  swapping (moving non-critical cells to a higher-Vt, smaller/slower
  library) can *reduce* both area and leakage power on non-critical paths
  without touching timing.
- **Area ↔ Power**: larger cells (upsized for timing) generally have higher
  input capacitance and thus higher dynamic power on their output nets —
  cross-check any upsizing decision against `report-power.md`'s switching
  power section before accepting it as "free."

## Format variants

The combinational/noncombinational/macro breakdown above is specific to
Design Compiler. There's no universal "area report" shape: Yosys (the
standard open-source synthesizer) has no `report_area` command at all —
its equivalent, `stat -liberty`, outputs a per-cell-type instance count
histogram plus a single "Chip area" total, with no combinational/
noncombinational/macro split and no buf/inv subset. See `see-also.md`.
